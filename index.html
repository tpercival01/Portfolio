About

Small software with a reason to exist.

Percival Studio is an independent software studio by Thomas Percival, building mobile apps, games and experiments across iPhone and Android. The aim is simple: useful or interesting software, kept focused and sold fairly.

Fair by default Free when that makes sense. Otherwise, a small one-time price.
No attention tax No ads. No subscriptions. No recurring charge just to keep using an app.
Focused on purpose Each product should have a reason to exist without becoming bloated for the sake of it.
