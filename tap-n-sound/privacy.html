Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Your privacy is simple here.

Tap n’ Sound does not collect, store, share or sell personal data.

The app does not require an account, login or profile.

Tap n’ Sound does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies or third-party data collection services.

App preferences such as sound, haptic strength, visual set and visual style are stored locally on your device.

No information is transmitted to Percival Studio or any third party through the app.

Children and families

Tap n’ Sound is designed to be a simple sensory experience and does not collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes to this policy

If Tap n’ Sound changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.
