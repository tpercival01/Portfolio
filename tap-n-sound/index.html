Tap n’ Sound

A simple sensory space built around tapping, colour, sound and haptic feedback.

Free. No ads. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases.

How it works

Tap anywhere.

Every touch creates a colourful letter, number or symbol, accompanied by a random sound and haptic feedback.

Tap slowly or as quickly as you like. Multiple touches, sounds and visuals can happen at the same time.

Choose between letters, numbers, symbols or everything together, adjust haptic strength, disable sound, and switch between modern and classic visual styles.
